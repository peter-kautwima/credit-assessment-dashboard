# Final review

A fresh read-only reviewer inspected the current source and tests after the scale wave, focusing on
windowing, keyboard/focus behavior, workflow status versus risk, Pending nulls and print.

## Finding

**Partial evidence was incorrectly collapsed into a Pending claim.** The credit and financial
panels treated any missing field as though the whole resource were absent. A Complete file with a
reported score but no risk band would therefore hide the score and say the assessment was Pending;
the bank-statement panel had the same defect.

## Resolution evidence

- Added a regression case with a Complete assessment, reported score and credits, but missing risk
  band and debits. It failed by hiding score 612 before the fix.
- Each reported field now renders independently. Missing fields say “not reported”; calculations
  whose inputs are incomplete say “Unavailable”.
- Pending wording is conditional on the assessment's actual workflow status.
- `npm run verify`: 32 tests passed and the production build completed.

No other material defect was found in the scale window, keyboard/focus paths, shipped Pending shape
or print rules during the static review.
